OUR STORY / 东方不败

Many backgrounds.
Common ground.

DFBB is a Boston nonprofit dance crew formed by young professionals from different fields. Dance is where our paths meet.

THE WAY WE MOVE

Built around a
shared love of dance.

From K-pop covers to hip-hop and jazz, we explore movement together and share it through performance and video.

Founded in 2016, DFBB has appeared in Boston-area cultural programming, including the HCSSA Spring Gala.
